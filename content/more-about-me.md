        Music has been a lifelong passion of mine. I began my musical journey as a childhood television performer and recording artist, frequently appearing on television, recording children's songs in professional studios, and participating in public service advertisements. Growing up in front of the camera and behind the microphone sparked a love for music and performance that continues to this day. Beyond singing, I enjoy playing the pipa, cello, piano, and ukulele.

        Giving back to the community is another important part of my life. Over the years, I've volunteered in a variety of settings, from supporting patients in the Emergency Department at NewYork-Presbyterian Hospital and assisting with ophthalmic examinations and imaging to encouraging young girls to explore science through Columbia University's Girls' Science Day. These experiences have been incredibly meaningful to me and have strengthened my appreciation for compassion, education, and community engagement.

        Today, I also enjoy hosting local charity events and supporting community initiatives. Combining my love for performing with my passion for volunteering has been especially rewarding, and I hope to continue finding meaningful ways to give back to the community.
